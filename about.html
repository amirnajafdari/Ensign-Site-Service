About Ensign Site Service

A trusted name in industrial site services — built on decades of engineering expertise, delivered by people who know the industry.

Who We Are

Engineering Expertise You Can Rely On

Ensign Site Service is a specialist industrial site services company with over two decades of experience. We work with clients across the UK to design, install and maintain critical industrial infrastructure — from complex pipework systems to fuel storage tanks.

Our team of engineers, designers and site technicians bring together deep technical knowledge and practical on-site experience. We pride ourselves on delivering to the highest standards of quality, safety and environmental responsibility.

Based in the UK, we operate nationally and are accredited by leading industry bodies including ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001 and OFTEC.

How We Work

Our Core Values

Safety First

Safety underpins every decision we make. We maintain strict HSE compliance, carry out thorough risk assessments, and ensure every member of our team is properly trained and equipped to work safely on site.

On Time Delivery

We understand that plant downtime costs money. Our project management approach prioritises realistic scheduling, proactive communication and efficient site coordination to keep your project on track.

Quality & Compliance

Every project is delivered to the highest technical standards. Our ISO 9001 quality management system ensures consistent, auditable processes from design through to final handover documentation.
